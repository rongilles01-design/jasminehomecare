GOOD TO KNOW

Pricing questions.

Is my online estimate the final price?

No. We confirm the property, service scope, condition, travel and applicable taxes before you approve your quote.

Can I start with recurring service?

Yes. We confirm initial cleaning needs and agree on a schedule. Maintenance discounts apply to subsequent eligible visits.

Do you offer custom packages?

Yes. Contact us for larger homes, special schedules or a custom decorating project.

How do I book and pay?

Request a quote first. We confirm availability, scope, price, payment and cancellation terms before you accept. No payment is collected by this estimator.
