WE RESPECT YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy information.

How this website handles information you choose to share.

Contact information

When you submit a quote request, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, contact details, city or ZIP code, requested services, preferred timing, and project description. The form is processed and stored through Netlify Forms, our website hosting provider. Netlify uses spam detection, which may involve Akismet, and rejects submissions caught by additional anti-spam checks. Authorized Jasmine team members can review submissions in Netlify and may receive email notifications if configured. We use these details to respond to your inquiry and discuss potential services. Please do not submit payment card numbers, medical details, or other sensitive information.

Direct communications

If you email or call Jasmine Interior Design & Housekeeping, we use the information you share to respond to your inquiry and discuss services. Email, telephone, website hosting, and spam-screening providers may process relevant information under their own terms and policies. We do not intentionally collect sensitive financial or health information through the quote form.

Third-party content

The website may load inspiration photos from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive standard technical information, such as IP address and browser details, under their own policies. Instagram is a separate website with its own privacy practices.

Cookies and analytics

This version of the site does not include analytics trackers or advertising pixels. Hosting providers may keep standard server access logs.

Retention and your choices

We may retain inquiries as needed to reply, manage customer relationships, prevent abuse, or meet applicable legal obligations. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of an inquiry, contact us using the email below; some records may need to be kept where legally required. Please review this notice whenever our website practices change.
