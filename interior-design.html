GOOD TO KNOW

Questions about styling?

Do I need to redesign my whole home?

Not at all. We can discuss a single room, a simple styling refresh, or a broader home project depending on your goals.

Can Jasmine work with furniture I already own?

Yes. Share what you have and what you’d like to change. We can explore how to make existing items work in a refreshed space.

Do you offer renovations or permitted construction?

Our advertised services focus on residential decorating, styling, and non-structural improvements. Structural work, permitted alterations, and any services requiring a separate professional license are not included.
