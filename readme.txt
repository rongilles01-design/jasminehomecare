# JASMINE INTERIOR DESIGN & HOUSEKEEPING — WEBSITE PACKAGE

A responsive 7-page static website for jasminehomecare.net.

## Pages
- index.html — homepage
- interior-design.html — decorating & styling
- housekeeping.html — cleaning services
- gallery.html — inspiration gallery (stock images, clearly identified)
- about.html — story and values
- contact.html — quote request form and contact details
- privacy.html — starter privacy information; review before publishing

## Preview
Open index.html in a web browser. Browse from the menu. (Images use the Unsplash CDN while local SVG illustrations are included as fallbacks if images are unavailable.)

## Publish to your domain
1. Make sure your web host supports uploading custom HTML/CSS/JS (e.g., traditional GoDaddy Web Hosting/cPanel, not necessarily GoDaddy Website Builder).
2. Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to your hosting public_html (or website root). Do not upload just the ZIP and expect it to open automatically.
3. Connect jasminehomecare.net to the hosting account, configure SSL/HTTPS, and verify every link.
4. Verify both phone numbers, mailbox Info@jasminehomecare.net, Instagram handle, approved services and areas served.
5. Replace inspiration photos with your own portfolio (and update captions) when available. Consider downloading/licensing photos and hosting them locally to avoid a third-party dependency.
6. Test on a mobile phone and desktop browser.

## IMPORTANT: Quote form
The form in contact.html is a *no-backend email-request form*: after a visitor clicks Prepare Email Request, their device will try to open their email application with the details filled in. The visitor MUST press Send there. The Copy Request button provides a backup. It does NOT silently send/store inquiries or guarantee form delivery.

For fully automated on-site submissions and notification emails, connect a verified form service or server-side handler through your host. Set it up with spam filtering and a privacy notice. Do not advertise it as automated until tested end to end.

## Website images / fonts
Images hotlink to images.unsplash.com and have local SVG fallbacks. If those remote images are not available, the site will show illustrated placeholders. Google Fonts also has a local serif/sans fallback. The gallery labels images as inspirational rather than completed projects.

## Design system
Ivory #f9f7f2, forest green #233a31, sage #94a18b, muted gold #a88e67. Fonts Playfair Display, DM Sans (web), with Georgia/Arial fallbacks.
